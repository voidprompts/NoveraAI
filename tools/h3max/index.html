H3Max

Generate short videos from text or images with configurable frame controls.

Official website
Overview

What H3Max does

H3Max is a browser-based workspace for creating 5–15 second AI videos from written directions or source images, with prompt expansion and optional first- and end-frame controls.

Decision context

Best fit

H3Max is most relevant to people who want to generate video from text or a source image. It is positioned in Video Generation; the official product should be checked against the exact workflow, data, and output requirements before adoption.

Capabilities

Key features

  • Generate video from text or a source image
  • Create clips between 5 and 15 seconds
  • Choose 480p or 768p output
  • Guide motion with optional first and end frames
Evaluation checklist

Before you choose

  • Check the current free-tier allowance, feature restrictions, watermark or export limits, and the cost of the expected paid usage.
  • Check output length, resolution, watermark, render-time, and export limits on the intended plan.
  • Confirm commercial-use rights for generated footage, voices, music, and uploaded assets.
  • Test motion consistency and editing control with a representative project before committing.

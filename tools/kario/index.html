What Kario does

Kario turns a written brief, product URL, logo, images, or product shots into an animated motion-graphics project. Unlike a flattened generated clip, the result keeps its text, shapes, media, keyframes, and timeline editable. Users can request revisions in plain language, adjust layers manually, connect Claude or ChatGPT through MCP, and export landscape or vertical MP4 files. A free plan includes monthly agent credits, with paid plans adding more credits and higher-resolution exports.

Key features

  • Create motion graphics from a brief, product URL, logo, or uploaded media
  • Edit generated text, shapes, images, video, audio, keyframes, and timing
  • Request design changes in plain language or adjust individual layers manually
  • Connect Claude or ChatGPT through MCP to build and remix projects from chat
  • Export MP4 video in landscape or vertical formats with plan-based resolution limits

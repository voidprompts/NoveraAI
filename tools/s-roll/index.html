What S-Roll does

S-Roll is a local AI clipping editor for long-form video on Apple silicon Macs. It transcribes and analyzes footage on-device, scores promising moments, and lets users describe a moment in chat to locate and cut it. Automatic subject tracking reframes clips for vertical video, while the editor supports trimming, captions, titles, and reusable brand presets before MP4 export. Videos, transcripts, chats, project names, and exports stay on the Mac; sign-in, updates, and the initial model download use the internet. The app is currently free with no watermark.

Key features

  • Score long videos and review candidate clips with titles, summaries, and previews
  • Find a specific action, moment, or topic by chatting with the video
  • Track a subject automatically while reframing footage for vertical clips
  • Trim cuts and style captions, titles, and reusable brand presets before export
  • Process source videos, transcripts, chats, and exported clips locally on Apple silicon Macs

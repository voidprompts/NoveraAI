What Floyi does

Floyi is a content-strategy platform for building topical authority across traditional and AI search. It carries brand and audience context through topical research, maps, content briefs, article drafts, optimization, publishing, and performance measurement. A free pay-as-you-go tier includes strategy essentials and limited credits, while Pro and Scale plans add production, auditing, visibility, team, and publishing features.

Key features

  • Create a reusable brand foundation and audience context
  • Build topical maps from search behavior and intent
  • Generate research-backed briefs and brand-aware article drafts
  • Track rankings, topical coverage, and visibility in AI search

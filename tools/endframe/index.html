EndFrame

Mac video editor where AI coding agents build editable scenes on a timeline.

Official website
Overview

What EndFrame does

EndFrame is a macOS video editor that lets Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, and other coding agents assemble launch videos, product demos, tutorials, and social clips. An agent can read a repository or use tagged images, clips, logos, and audio to build editable, code-driven scenes on a timeline. Users can revise frames, restore versions, and render 1080p or 4K exports locally. It is free during early access and currently requires Apple silicon with macOS 15 or later.

Decision context

Best fit

EndFrame is most relevant to people who want to build videos from a prompt, a product repository, or tagged media assets. It is positioned in Video Generation; the official product should be checked against the exact workflow, data, and output requirements before adoption.

Capabilities

Key features

  • Build videos from a prompt, a product repository, or tagged media assets
  • Create editable code-driven scenes, typography, charts, and transitions
  • Render frames for agent review and check them against a 33-rule quality gate
  • Trim and reorder timeline clips or restore an earlier version of a project
  • Export locally in 1080p or 4K on a compatible Apple silicon Mac
Evaluation checklist

Before you choose

  • Confirm the current license, included usage, hosting requirements, and whether paid services are needed for production use.
  • Check output length, resolution, watermark, render-time, and export limits on the intended plan.
  • Confirm commercial-use rights for generated footage, voices, music, and uploaded assets.
  • Test motion consistency and editing control with a representative project before committing.

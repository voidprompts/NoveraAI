Overview

What Suno does

Suno is an AI music creation platform for turning prompts and lyrics into full musical tracks.

Decision context

Best fit

Suno is most relevant to people who want to create complete songs from prompts. It is positioned in Audio & Music; the official product should be checked against the exact workflow, data, and output requirements before adoption.

Capabilities

Key features

  • Create complete songs from prompts
  • Generate vocals and instrumentation
  • Explore genres and musical moods
  • Extend and refine song ideas
Evaluation checklist

Before you choose

  • Check the current free-tier allowance, feature restrictions, watermark or export limits, and the cost of the expected paid usage.
  • Confirm voice-consent, licensing, attribution, and commercial-use requirements.
  • Compare export formats, audio quality, language support, and generation limits.
  • Review the provider’s rules for uploaded recordings and cloned or synthetic voices.

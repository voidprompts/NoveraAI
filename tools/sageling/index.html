Overview

What Sageling does

Sageling is a free local AI assistant for Apple silicon Macs with at least 16 GB of memory. It runs its model on the computer, keeps conversations, files, and memories on the Mac, and accesses the internet only when a requested task needs it. The assistant can work with local documents, transcribe media, research information, and complete longer multi-step tasks without cloud usage limits.
